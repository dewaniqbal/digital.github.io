# Follow-Up Email Guide

## After applying

```text
Subject: Follow-up on [Role] application

Hi [Name],

I hope you are well. I recently applied for the [Role] position at [Company] and wanted to briefly follow up. My background in [skill/domain] aligns with the role's focus on [requirement], especially my experience with [specific proof].

I would be grateful for the chance to be considered and am happy to share any additional information.

Best,
[Name]
```

## After recruiter screen

```text
Subject: Thank you — [Role]

Hi [Name],

Thank you for speaking with me about the [Role] position today. I appreciated learning more about [specific topic]. The conversation strengthened my interest because my experience in [specific match] seems relevant to what the team needs.

Please let me know if I can share anything else before the next step.

Best,
[Name]
```

## After interview

```text
Subject: Thank you — [Role] interview

Hi [Name],

Thank you for taking the time to speak with me about the [Role] position. I enjoyed discussing [topic] and learning more about [team/project].

After our conversation, I am even more interested in the opportunity to contribute to [specific outcome]. My experience with [proof point] feels especially relevant to the work you described.

Thanks again,
[Name]
```

## Rules

- Keep follow-ups short.
- Mention one specific conversation point when possible.
- Do not pressure the recipient.
- Do not imply an offer or next step unless it was confirmed.
