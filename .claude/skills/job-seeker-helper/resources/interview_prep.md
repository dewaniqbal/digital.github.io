# Interview Prep Guide

## Goal

Prepare the candidate for likely interviews based on the role, company, and job description.

## Interview prep pack sections

1. Role summary
2. Candidate positioning statement
3. Recruiter screen prep
4. Hiring manager interview prep
5. Technical or portfolio prep
6. Behavioral STAR story bank
7. Questions to ask interviewers
8. Salary and negotiation talking points
9. Weak spots and honest bridging language

## Candidate positioning statement

Use 30 to 45 seconds:

```text
I am a [role identity] with experience in [top relevant areas]. Recently, I worked on [specific example], where I used [tools/methods] to [business outcome]. For this role, I see a strong match with [JD requirement 1], [JD requirement 2], and [JD requirement 3].
```

## STAR answer format

- Situation: one sentence
- Task: one sentence
- Action: 2 to 4 sentences
- Result: one sentence with metric if true
- Reflection: one sentence about what was learned

## Weak spot bridging

If the candidate lacks a requirement:

```text
I have not used [specific tool] in a production setting yet, but I have done similar work with [related tool/method]. My approach would be to ramp quickly by [learning plan], while relying on my existing experience in [transferable skill].
```

Do not advise the user to pretend experience they do not have.

## Questions to ask interviewers

Include questions about:

- success in first 90 days
- team priorities
- data, tools, or workflow
- collaboration style
- role expectations
- hiring process next steps
